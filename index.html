PeoplePay

Pay people by tapping

PeoplePay is a Bitcoin SV wallet for Android where only you hold your keys. Send, receive and pay each other with a tap of a card or a phone.

  • Your recovery phrase never leaves your phone.
  • Every card payment is approved with your fingerprint.
  • A PeoplePay card holds no money and no wallet key.
